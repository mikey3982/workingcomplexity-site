## 2. Brand essence

**Name.** Working Complexity. Always one word when set as the logotype: `workingcomplexity`. Two words in running prose and in legal/formal contexts: "Working Complexity". Domain is `workingcomplexity.health`.

**What WC is.** An open working practice — a space for thinking about complex healthcare in public, working *with* complexity rather than reducing it. Not an agency taking briefs. Our readers are the people a practice like this might one day serve — NHS, charities, pharma, commissioners — but the relationship today is a reader's, not a client's. What Working Complexity becomes commercially is still being worked out, and we say so.

**The thesis (the sentence under everything we produce).**

> The healthcare system doesn't need to be simpler. It needs to be better understood.

**The three dimensions (the working model).** Every engagement draws on three inseparable capacities:

1. **Understand** — deep thinking, sense-making, systems and complexity theory.
2. **Make** — move from insight to things that can be tried and tested in real conditions, not left as abstraction.
3. **Feel** — empathy, lived experience, human depth.

**The manifesto line.**

> The patient is not an edge case. The family is not overhead. The clinician's uncertainty is not a failure. *These are the system.*

**Audience.** Senior healthcare decision-makers: NHS executives, commissioners, charity leaders, pharma medical/patient-experience teams. They are intelligent, time-poor, and sceptical of consultancy boilerplate. Write for them.

## 3. Voice and tone

WC writes like a literate, unhurried practitioner — not like a consultancy, not like a startup, not like a think tank. Our voice carries authority without performing it.

### 3.1 Principles

1. **Hold complexity, don't flatten it.** Never reach for "simply" or "just" as a rhetorical move. If something is hard, say it is hard, then show how we work with it.
2. **Short, paired statements.** WC's signature cadence is two short sentences in counterpoint. *"Not simplified. Understood." · "Emergent by design. Rigorous by necessity." · "Low fidelity where appropriate; high fidelity where it matters."*
3. **Concrete over abstract.** Name the clinician, the ward, the handoff, the moment. Avoid "stakeholders", "end users", "solutions", "leverage".
4. **Evidence-anchored, plainly stated.** Cite what we know; flag what we don't. Never use false precision.
5. **Respect the reader's intelligence.** No explainers for things a senior NHS reader already knows.
6. **UK English.** *Organisation, centre, behaviour, programme, analyse.*
7. **Em dashes, used well.** The em dash is part of WC's rhythm — use it to hold two ideas in relation, not as a pause-of-convenience.
8. **First person plural.** "We" not "the team" or "WC". The company speaks as a working practice, not as a brand.

### 3.2 Vocabulary

**Use freely:** *lived, emergent, assemblage, pathway, pilot, prototype, sense-making, uncertainty, honour, hold, map, the situation, the system, the person, the family, the clinician, evidence, rigour, inquiry, tension, leverage points, partial information.*

**Avoid:**

| Avoid | Reason | Prefer |
|---|---|---|
| solution, solutioning | consultancy cliché | approach, work, design |
| stakeholder | reduces people to roles | patient, clinician, family, commissioner (name them) |
| leverage (as verb) | managerial filler | use, draw on, put to work |
| unlock, supercharge, reimagine | startup vocabulary | — (rewrite) |
| simply, just | flattens complexity | — (delete) |
| end-to-end, holistic, seamless | buzzwords | — (rewrite concretely) |
| touchpoint | treats care as a funnel | moment, encounter, handoff |
| journey (overused) | fine in "care journey"; avoid "transformation journey" | pathway, process |
| AI-powered, data-driven | marketing | evidence-based, where applicable |
| deliverable | transactional | artefact, report, pilot, prototype |
| best-in-class, world-class | unearned | — (delete) |

### 3.3 Tone register

- **Reports and proposals:** considered, specific, evidence-led. First-person plural. No exclamation marks.
- **Decks:** same register, tighter. Headlines are full sentences, not labels. Never end a headline with a period *unless* it is one of the paired-statement devices.
- **Web and marketing copy:** slightly more declarative; the manifesto and thesis voice.
- **Emails and short notes:** plain, warm, unadorned. No sign-off flourishes.

### 3.4 Worked examples

> **Bad:** "We leverage cutting-edge systems thinking to unlock transformational outcomes for healthcare stakeholders."
>
> **Good:** "We map the full assemblage of a situation — its actors, its tensions, its hidden structures — before we propose anything."

> **Bad:** "Our end-to-end journey maps simplify the patient experience."
>
> **Good:** "We map the emotional terrain of a care pathway as precisely as the clinical one."

> **Bad:** "Innovative AI-powered solutions for NHS transformation."
>
> **Good:** "Designing at pace, testing in real conditions, adapting sensitively. Work that holds under the pressure of everyday care."

### 3.5 Headline grammar

- Sentence case for long headlines: *"Healthcare is not a widget. It is lived."*
- Paired-statement headlines take a period on each part: *"Emergent by design. Rigorous by necessity."*
- Short labels (UI, section tags, eyebrows) are UPPERCASE in Space Mono, letter-spaced (see §6).
- Never use title case. Never use ALL CAPS for running text.

## 4. Logo

### 4.1 Wordmark

The primary identifier is the wordmark **`workingcomplexity`** set in **Space Mono Bold (700)**, as one lowercase word, no space, no hyphen.

Optional subline: **`— .health`** set in Space Mono, smaller (≈0.8× wordmark), coloured gold `#C4A46B`, all caps, letter-spacing `0.2em`. The em dash is part of the mark — not a regular hyphen.

**Construction specs:**

- Wordmark: Space Mono 700, letter-spacing `0.08em`.
- Subline: Space Mono 400, letter-spacing `0.2em`, uppercase, rendered as `— .HEALTH`.
- Stack the subline directly under the wordmark, left-aligned, 2px gap (scale proportionally).

### 4.2 Logo mark

The **primary visual symbol** of Working Complexity. Three nested pointy-top hexagons with a centred dot — a datum mark that encodes the practice's core logic: system, method, craft, and person. This is the official logo mark; use it wherever a standalone symbol is needed.

File: `logo.svg` in the project root.

- Inner hex: stroke `#5B9BD5`. Represents the craft.

### 4.3 Lockups

Four approved lockups:

1. **Wordmark only** — default for most applications.
2. **Wordmark + subline** — for web headers, deck title slides, report covers.
3. **Mark + wordmark, horizontal** — mark on the left, wordmark on the right, vertically centred on the mark's horizontal midline. Gap between mark and wordmark = 0.5× mark diameter.
4. **Mark + wordmark, stacked** — mark above, wordmark below, centred. Use on square formats (social avatars, favicons at larger sizes).

Do **not** invent new lockups. Do not rearrange.

### 4.4 Clear space

Minimum clear space around any logo lockup = the x-height of the wordmark (the height of a lowercase `o`). Double that for cover pages and external advertising.

### 4.5 Minimum sizes

- Wordmark in print: **14pt** minimum.
- Wordmark on screen: **11px** minimum.
- Mark alone: **16px** minimum (without crosshair below 32px).

### 4.6 Do / don't

- **Do** place logos on the dark navy background (`#060D1B`) by default.
- **Do** use the light variant (ink-on-cream, see §5.4) for printed reports that must photocopy well.
- **Do not** use the wordmark in any font other than Space Mono Bold.
- **Do not** capitalise the wordmark, add a space, or add `.com`.
- **Do not** place the logo on a busy photographic background. If necessary, place on a solid brand colour panel.
- **Do not** recolour the mark outside the palette in §5.
- **Do not** rotate, skew, or apply effects (drop shadows, glows, bevels, outlines).

WC is **dark-first**. The default background is deep blueprint navy. A light variant exists for print economy.

| Text, dim | Dim Blue | `#3E5870` | 62, 88, 112 | Annotations, footnotes, document codes. |

### 5.2 Usage ratios (the 70 / 25 / 4 / 1 rule)

- ~70% background navy (any of the three navies).
- ~25% text (Paper Light + Muted Blue).
- ~4% Blueprint Blue (lines, diagrams, structural accents).
- ~1% Brass Gold. Gold is scarce on purpose — one word in a headline, a single ring, a CTA.

### 5.3 Accessibility

- Paper Light (`#E0E8F4`) on Blueprint Navy (`#060D1B`) passes WCAG AAA for normal text.
- Muted Blue (`#7A9ABF`) on Blueprint Navy passes WCAG AA for normal text — use for secondary copy only.
- Dim Blue (`#3E5870`) on navy **fails** AA for body text. Use only for annotations, document codes, decorative marks — never for content the reader must read.
- Brass Gold on Blueprint Navy passes AA for large text and UI. Do not set long body copy in gold.

### 5.4 Light variant (for print economy)

When a deliverable must photocopy, fax, or be printed in low-ink environments, use the inverted palette:

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background | `#F4F1E8` (warm paper) |
| Text, body | `#060D1B` |
| Text, muted | `#3E5870` |
| Accent primary | `#7A5A2A` (deepened brass, for AA contrast) |
| Accent secondary | `#1E4A7A` (deepened blue) |
| Rule, hairline | `#C8C0A8` |

The mark uses deepened brass and deepened blue on light backgrounds. Do not place pure `#C4A46B` or `#5B9BD5` on a light background — the contrast is insufficient.

## 6. Typography

Two typefaces. No substitutions.

### 6.1 Typefaces

- **Inter** — Display, headlines, body, captions, UI. Weights in active use: 300 (light), 400 (regular), 500 (medium), 700 (bold), 800 (extra bold).
- **Space Mono** — Labels, eyebrows, document codes, annotations, logo wordmark, technical tags. Weights: 400 (regular), 700 (bold). Italic 400 available for rare use.

Fallbacks (for environments without the typefaces): `Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` for Inter; `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, monospace` for Space Mono.

### 6.2 Hierarchy

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Inter | 800 | 3–6 rem | 44–72 | — | `-0.03em` | Sentence |
| H1 | Inter | 800 | 2.5 rem | 36 | 24 | `-0.03em` | Sentence |
| H2 | Inter | 700 | 1.75 rem | 28 | 18 | `-0.025em` | Sentence |
| H3 | Inter | 700 | 1.25 rem | 22 | 14 | `-0.02em` | Sentence |
| Body | Inter | 300–400 | 1 rem | 18 | 11 | `0` | Sentence |
| Body emphasis | Inter | 500 | — | — | — | `0` | Sentence |
| Eyebrow / Label | Space Mono | 400 | 0.6 rem | 9 | 8 | `0.25em` | UPPERCASE |
| Nav / Tag | Space Mono | 400 | 0.7 rem | 10 | 9 | `0.12em` | UPPERCASE |

| Document code | Space Mono | 700 | 0.62 rem | 9 | 8 | `0.22em` | UPPERCASE |

### 6.3 Casing and tracking rules

- **Headlines and body:** sentence case. Never title case.
- **Space Mono labels:** UPPERCASE with wide letter-spacing (0.1em minimum, 0.25em for short labels). Never UPPERCASE in Inter outside of acronyms.
- **Inter light (300)** is used for the *em-phrased* word in a headline, coloured gold. Example: `Healthcare is not a widget. **It is lived.**` where "It is lived." is Inter 300 in gold.
- **Line height:** 1.0 for display headlines, 1.2 for H1–H2, 1.35–1.4 for H3, 1.7–1.85 for body. Never set body tighter than 1.6.

### 6.4 Emphasis within body

- Bold sparingly. Bold a noun phrase, not a sentence.
- Italic for titles of works, and for the occasional phrase of voice-based emphasis.
- Never underline for emphasis. Underline is for links.
- Gold-colouring a word inside body copy is permitted only for pull quotes and the manifesto; never in ordinary paragraphs.

## 7. Graphic language

WC's visual register is **architectural blueprint meets data network**: hex grids, corner marks, dimension ticks, annotations, and hexagonal geometry throughout. It is never "medical illustration".

### 7.1 The hex grid

The foundational background texture is a **pointy-top hexagonal grid** — replacing the former rectilinear line grid. It reads as a data network or molecular lattice: ordered, technical, alive.

**Specification:**

- Hex orientation: pointy-top (vertices pointing up and down).
- Hex size (centre to vertex): **40px** for the major grid; **10px** for minor vertex markers.
- Major hex stroke: `rgba(74, 144, 226, 0.05)`, stroke-width `0.5px`.
- Minor vertex dots: `rgba(74, 144, 226, 0.025)`, radius `0.8px`, at each hex vertex of the minor grid.
- The major and minor grids are used together; the minor dots sit at the intersections of the hex lattice.

**SVG pattern reference:**

```svg

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
  <defs>
    <pattern id="wc-hex-major" width="69.28" height="120"
             patternUnits="userSpaceOnUse">
      <!-- Two hexagons offset to tile the plane -->
      <polygon points="34.64,0 69.28,20 69.28,60 34.64,80 0,60 0,20"
        stroke="rgba(74,144,226,0.05)" stroke-width="0.5" fill="none"/>
      <polygon points="34.64,80 69.28,100 69.28,140 34.64,160 0,140 0,100"
        stroke="rgba(74,144,226,0.05)" stroke-width="0.5" fill="none"/>
    </pattern>
  </defs>
  <rect width="100%" height="100%" fill="url(#wc-hex-major)"/>

```

**Usage rules:**

- Visible as a subtle overlay on covers, section dividers, and title slides.
- **Invisible** (opacity 0) on body content slides and report text pages.
- Increase to `rgba(74, 144, 226, 0.08)` on hero/poster applications where a stronger lattice is intentional.
- Column layout grid (8-column at 1920×1080, 12-column for A4 landscape) remains as a construction aid layered on top of the hex background; it is never made visible.

### 7.2 Corner marks

Each major composition (cover, title slide, section divider) carries four L-shaped corner marks in Dim Blue (`#3E5870`) at 40% opacity. Marks are 20px legs, 1px stroke. They frame the page without boxing it.

### 7.3 Dimension ticks and annotations

Short perpendicular ticks on diagram rules, matched with a Space Mono annotation. Annotations read like blueprint callouts:

- `WC-###  /  [SUBJECT]  /  REV.[A|B|C]` — document code, top-left of covers and section dividers.
- `SCALE 1:1  ·  ALL DIMENSIONS IN EXPERIENCE` — ironic-literal signature line, bottom-right of hero/cover compositions. Use once per artefact, never twice.
- Numbered sequences: `01 / 03`, `02 / 03`, `03 / 03` — for sets of three pillars, phases, or principles.
- Service codes: `SVC-01`, `SVC-02` — for service cards.
- Section codes: `§01`, `§02` — for report sections.

### 7.4 Iconography

All icons are constructed from the same primitives: hexagons, circles, rectangles, crosshairs, dimension ticks. They live on a **52-unit grid** with a **pointy-top hexagonal outer frame** as the standard container.

**Hex frame spec (52-unit grid):**

- Outer hex: pointy-top, size (centre-to-vertex) = 26 units. Stroke Blueprint Blue `#5B9BD5`, weight 0.8.
- Icon geometry sits within the inner 36-unit circle, leaving a clear border zone.
- One element — the datum point or focal symbol — is marked in Brass Gold, weight 0.8.

```svg
<!-- Icon hex frame, 52×52 viewBox, pointy-top -->
<svg viewBox="0 0 52 52" xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg">
  <polygon points="26,0 51.98,13 51.98,39 26,52 0.02,39 0.02,13"
    stroke="#5B9BD5" stroke-width="0.8" fill="none"/>
  <!-- Icon geometry here -->
</svg>
```

**Rules:**

- Icons are **line only** — no fills except for centre dots and the gold datum point.
- Two-colour max: Blueprint Blue for structure, Brass Gold for the point of focus.
- Never use pictographic icons (e.g. a heart for "empathy", a gear for "systems"). If an icon's meaning is not obvious, use a label instead.
- Strip the hex frame when icons are used at annotation scale (below 20px); retain only the inner geometry.

### 7.5 Hexagonal design language

Hexagons are WC's primary geometric unit. They reference data networks, molecular structure, and engineering lattices — all appropriate to a practice that maps complex systems. Every hex in the system is **pointy-top**.

**The four roles of the hexagon:**

**1. Background texture** — the hex grid (§7.1). Structural, faint, always present on covers and dividers.

**2. Icon frame** — the 52-unit hex container (§7.4). Wraps every icon in the system.

**3. Card and callout decoration** — rectangular cards are **not** hexagon-shaped, but carry hex geometry at their edges:
  - A partial hex outline (one or two sides of a hexagon, clipped at the card boundary) in Blueprint Blue at 30% opacity marks the top-left or bottom-right corner of a callout panel.
  - Eyebrow labels for callouts are preceded by a 24px hex shape (Blueprint Blue, 0.8px stroke, no fill) rather than the standard gold rule — or both may be used together.
  - The hex partial is generated from the same 40px-size hex as the major grid, anchored so it appears as a natural lattice node.

**4. Structural accent** — hexagons used as standalone graphic devices on section dividers and covers:
  - One large, near-empty hex (size 120–200px, stroke Blueprint Blue at 12% opacity) may sit in the background of a section-divider slide, slightly off-centre, behind other content.
  - Small solid hex fills (`#C4A46B`, opacity 60%, size 6px) may mark data points and list bullets in place of round dots.
  - Hex-shaped "node" markers (Blueprint Blue, stroke only, 12px) may appear at the intersections of diagram lines to suggest a network structure.

**Hex sizing ladder** (use these sizes only; do not invent intermediate sizes):

| Role | Size (centre-to-vertex) |
|---|---|
| Background grid | 40px |
| Icon frame | 26 units on 52-unit icon grid |
| Card accent partial | 40px (same as grid, so it aligns) |
| Section divider large | 120–200px |
| Data point bullet | 6px |
| Diagram node marker | 12px |

**Rules:**
- Never fill a large hex with solid colour. Hex fills are reserved for tiny data-point bullets only.
- Never nest hexagons inside each other except in the official logo mark (§4.2); that construction is reserved for the mark alone.
- Never rotate a hexagon off the pointy-top axis. All hexes are vertically aligned.
- Do not use hexagons on body content slides or report pages where they are not a functional element.

### 7.6 Photography

Photography is **rare and high-bar**. When used:

- Documentary, not staged. Available-light interiors, hospitals and clinical spaces photographed without actors.
- Duotone treatment: shadows pushed to Blueprint Navy, highlights to Paper Light. Brass Gold reserved for a single accent.
- No clichés: no stethoscopes, no gloved hands, no stock-library smiling clinicians, no people in business attire around a laptop.
- People are photographed with dignity, from behind or in environment, not as "the patient" or "the carer" personas.

If suitable photography is not available, **do not** substitute with stock. Use the hex graphic language instead.

### 7.7 Diagrams

Diagrams are the primary illustrative form at WC. Standards:

- Strokes in Blueprint Blue; one element in Brass Gold to mark the datum or focus.
- Labels in Space Mono UPPERCASE, letter-spaced.
- No drop shadows, gradients (except the one pillar-accent gradient defined in the CSS), or 3-D effects.
- Every diagram carries a document code and a one-line caption in Space Mono Dim Blue.
- Hex node markers (12px, Blueprint Blue, stroke only) may appear at connection points within network or process diagrams.

## 8. Signature devices

These are the "tells" that make an artefact unmistakably WC. Use at least two on every long-form deliverable.

1. **Document code in the top-left** of covers and section dividers: `WC-### / SUBJECT / REV.A`.
2. **The scale line** `SCALE 1:1 · ALL DIMENSIONS IN EXPERIENCE` bottom-right of the cover.
3. **A paired-statement headline** somewhere in the first three pages. *"Emergent by design. Rigorous by necessity."*
4. **The logo mark** (§4.2 — three nested hexagons) at least once per document, or a structural diagram that references its geometry.
5. **Corner marks** on at least the cover and each section divider.
6. **An eyebrow above every major headline** — Space Mono UPPERCASE label in Brass Gold, prefixed by a 32px gold rule.
7. **Numbered sequences** for grouped content (`01 / 03`, `SVC-01`, etc.).
8. **One word of a headline in Inter 300 gold**, to break the 800-weight mass.
9. **The hex grid background** on covers and section dividers — the lattice is always present at the specified low opacity, never removed.
10. **A hex structural accent** somewhere in the composition — one of: a large background hex on a section divider, hex node markers in a diagram, or a hex-framed callout. Use exactly one per composition; never cluster hex accents.

## 9. Application: Word reports

Word reports are the default for long-form written deliverables (research outputs, strategy documents, briefings). They are produced to be read, printed, and annotated.

### 9.1 Page setup

- Format: A4 portrait, 25mm margins all sides.
- Background: **light variant** (§5.4). Word reports print; dark backgrounds waste ink and photocopy badly.
- Body font: Inter 11pt, line height 1.5.
- Headings: Inter — H1 24pt 800, H2 18pt 700, H3 14pt 700, H4 11pt 700.
- Labels and captions: Space Mono 8pt, letter-spaced 0.15em, UPPERCASE.

### 9.2 Cover

- Document code top-left: `WC-### / [SUBJECT] / REV.A` in Space Mono 8pt.
- Title: Inter 32pt 800, sentence case, left-aligned. Two-line max. One word may be set in Inter 300 deepened brass.
- Subtitle: Inter 14pt 400, Muted Blue (print-variant), directly below title, max three lines.
- The logo mark (§4.2, light-variant colours) bottom-left at 40mm diameter.
- `hello@workingcomplexity.health · workingcomplexity.health` bottom-right in Space Mono 8pt.
- Four corner marks.

### 9.3 Section structure

- Every section opens with a section divider page: section number `§0#`, section title in Inter 24pt, a one-sentence precis in Inter 14pt 300.
- Running header from page 2 onward: document code left, subject centre, page number right — all in Space Mono 7pt Dim Blue.
- Footer: `Working Complexity · workingcomplexity.health` centred, Space Mono 7pt Dim Blue.

### 9.4 Paragraph styles

- Body paragraphs: 11pt, line height 1.5, no first-line indent, 6pt space after.
- Pull quotes: Inter 18pt 700, left-ruled with a 2pt deepened-brass line, 24pt space before and after.
- Lists: 11pt, 4pt space between items. Bullet character: `—` (en dash), not the default Word dot.
- Callouts: Card Navy panel (use the light-variant card `#E8E4D4`), 12pt padding all sides, eyebrow in Space Mono 8pt brass, body in Inter 11pt.

### 9.5 Tables and figures

- Tables: no vertical rules. Single horizontal rule top and bottom in Dim Blue, and below the header row. Header row in Inter 10pt 700.
- Every figure has a caption in Space Mono 8pt UPPERCASE: `FIG. 01 · [TITLE]` on the first line, plain-case description in Inter 9pt Muted Blue on the second.
- Diagrams follow §7.7.

### 9.6 References and footnotes

- Footnotes in Inter 9pt, line height 1.4.
- Reference style: author-date (Harvard), UK English.
- In-text citations: `(Author, Year)` — never superscript numerals for academic material.

### 10.1 Long-form reports (print-ready)

### 10.2 Presentation PDFs (digital-first)

Used when a deck is distributed as a PDF (leave-behinds, board packs).

## 11. Application: PowerPoint decks

Decks are WC's primary client-facing artefact for live presentation.

### 11.1 Deck setup

- Format: 16:9, 1920×1080px (or 13.33" × 7.5").
- Background: Blueprint Navy (`#060D1B`) default.
- Safe margin: 72px all sides. Content sits on an 8-column grid, 24px gutter.
- Four corner marks on title and section-divider slides only; **no** corner marks on content slides.

### 11.2 Slide masters

Six masters, no more. Every slide in a WC deck is one of these:

**Master A — Title slide**
- Document code top-left: `WC-### / [SUBJECT] / REV.A`, Space Mono 10pt Dim Blue.
- Eyebrow: gold rule + Space Mono UPPERCASE label (e.g. `BOARD BRIEFING · 2026-04-21`).
- Title: Inter 64pt 800, sentence case, left-aligned, up to three lines. One word may be Inter 300 gold.
- Subtitle: Inter 20pt 400 Muted Blue, below title, max two lines.
- Logo mark (§4.2) bottom-right at 120px diameter, with crosshair.
- Bottom-left: `workingcomplexity.health`, Space Mono 10pt.
- Bottom-right: `SCALE 1:1 · ALL DIMENSIONS IN EXPERIENCE`, Space Mono 8pt Dim Blue (optional, use once per deck).

**Master B — Section divider**
- Section code: `§0#`, Space Mono 14pt Brass Gold, top-left of content area.
- Section title: Inter 48pt 800, left-aligned.
- Precis: one sentence, Inter 20pt 300 Muted Blue.
- Numbered sequence annotation (e.g. `PART 02 / 05`) in Space Mono 10pt top-right.
- Faint blueprint grid visible at 4% opacity.

**Master C — Content slide**
- Eyebrow: gold 32px rule + Space Mono UPPERCASE label, top of content.
- Headline: Inter 32pt 700, sentence case, max two lines.
- Body: one of — a single paragraph in Inter 18pt 400; a list of up to five items; a two-column layout; a single diagram.
- Never put both a long paragraph and a diagram on the same slide. If it does not fit, split the slide.

**Master D — Quote / manifesto slide**
- Centred composition on Deep Navy.
- Quote: Inter 36pt 700, centred, max six lines. One word may be Inter 800 gold.
- Attribution: Space Mono 10pt UPPERCASE Dim Blue, below quote, preceded by an em dash.
- Optional faint logo mark or hex lattice background at 7% opacity.

**Master E — Diagram slide**
- Diagram centred, occupying ~70% of slide width.
- Label in Space Mono 10pt UPPERCASE above diagram.
- One-line caption in Inter 14pt 300 Muted Blue below.
- No other body copy.

**Master F — Closing slide**
- Wordmark + subline centred at 40% slide width.
- `hello@workingcomplexity.health` and `workingcomplexity.health` in Inter 18pt Paper Light, centred below wordmark with 48px gap.
- Logo mark (§4.2), bottom-centre at 80px diameter.

### 11.3 Charts

- Bar and column charts: Blueprint Blue fills, Brass Gold to mark the one bar that matters (the datum). No chart junk.
- Line charts: Blueprint Blue for secondary series, Brass Gold for primary. Line weight 2pt. No markers unless data points are discrete.
- Axes: Dim Blue hairlines, Space Mono 10pt labels.
- Never use stacked bar charts with more than three segments.
- Never use pie charts. Use a horizontal bar chart or a number statement instead.

### 11.4 Numbers and statistics

Hero numbers (single-metric slides) are set in Inter 128pt 800 Brass Gold, left-aligned, with a Space Mono 14pt label above and a one-line Inter 20pt 400 caption below.

### 11.5 Animation and transitions

None. Content cuts between slides. No builds, no fades, no motion paths. A slide is a page. (Video has its own motion rules — see §13.7.)

### 11.6 Slide count and cadence

- Cover → section dividers → content (2–5 per section) → quote (at most once per 10 slides) → closer.
- Average deck length: 15–25 slides. If a deck exceeds 30, it is almost certainly a report in the wrong format. Offer to convert.

## 13. Application: Video

Video is the one WC artefact that runs in time. It uses the same palette, type and graphic language as the deck, but motion is allowed here because, in time, motion carries meaning. A deck is a page; a video is a drawing being made. All sizes below are given as a percentage of frame height (**H**); pixel values are for 1920×1080.

### 13.1 Canvas, formats and frame rate

| Format | Size | Use | Safe margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16:9 (default) | 1920×1080 | Web, LinkedIn, YouTube, presentations | 72px all sides (6.7% H) |
| 9:16 | 1080×1920 | Shorts, Reels, stories | 72px sides; top 10% H and bottom 20% H kept clear of text (platform UI) |
| 1:1 (optional) | 1080×1080 | Feed posts only | 72px all sides |

- Frame rate: **25 fps** by default; **30 fps** when the source footage or a screen recording is 30. Never mix rates in one timeline.
- Export: H.264 MP4, Rec.709, 1080p, at least 12 Mbps so the faint hex grid does not band.
- Title, section and end cards sit on Blueprint Navy `#060D1B`. Deep Navy `#0B1629` may be used for quote cards.
- Each video carries a document code in the standard form: `WC-### / [SUBJECT] / REV.A`.
- Minimum on-screen text size: 1.6% H (≈17px). Nothing smaller, including annotations.

### 13.2 Captions

Captions are verbatim subtitles of what is said — not summaries, not highlights.

- **Type:** Inter 500, **4.4% H** (≈48px) on 16:9 and 1:1; **3.0% H** (≈58px) on 9:16. Line height 1.3. Sentence case as spoken — never all caps.
- **Colour:** Paper Light `#E0E8F4` on a Blueprint Navy band at **72% opacity** — `rgba(6, 13, 27, 0.72)`. Band padding 0.8% H vertical, 1.2% H horizontal; square corners.
- **Length:** 1–2 lines; maximum **42 characters per line** on 16:9 (32 on 9:16 and 1:1). Break at natural phrase boundaries, never between article and noun.
- **Position:** centred, bottom-anchored; bottom of the band **8% H** above the frame edge on 16:9 and 1:1, **22% H** on 9:16.
- **Timing:** on screen for at least 1.2 s and no more than 7 s; reading rate no faster than 17 characters per second. Captions cut on and off with the speech — no fades, no word-by-word pop-on, no karaoke highlighting.
- **Text:** UK spelling in captions even when the speaker's accent is American (*organisation, programme*). No emoji. Clean filler words (*um, er*) only where they carry no meaning; never change what was said.
- Always also deliver a sidecar `.srt` so platforms can show their own captions.

### 13.3 Lower-thirds

One per speaker, on their first appearance only.

- **Eyebrow (role):** Space Mono 400, UPPERCASE, **1.8% H** (≈19px), letter-spacing `0.25em`, Brass Gold `#C4A46B`. E.g. `CONSULTANT RESPIRATORY PHYSICIAN`.
- **Name:** Inter 700, **4.0% H** (≈43px), Paper Light, tracking `-0.02em`, sentence case. Optional third line for the organisation: Inter 400, 2.6% H, Muted Blue `#7A9ABF`.
- **Rule (optional):** a 1px (0.1% H) Blueprint Blue `#5B9BD5` hairline under the name, the width of the name. It draws on from left to right.
- **Position:** left-aligned to the 72px safe margin; bottom of the block at **76% H** from the top, so it never collides with captions. On busy footage, back it with the same 72% navy band as captions.
- **Timing:** enters over 400 ms, holds **4 s** (100 frames at 25 fps, 120 at 30 fps), exits over 300 ms.

### 13.4 Title, section and end cards

**Title card (opens the video)** — the deck's Master A, in time.
- Document code top-left: Space Mono 700, 1.6% H, `0.22em`, Dim Blue `#3E5870`.
- Eyebrow: 32px gold rule + Space Mono UPPERCASE label, 1.8% H, Brass Gold.
- Title: Inter 800, **6.5% H** (≈70px), `-0.03em`, sentence case, max two lines. One word may be Inter 300 gold.
- Logo mark (§4.2) bottom-right at 11% H (≈120px), with crosshair. Hex grid at the §7.1 opacity. Four corner marks.
- Hold 3–4 s after the last element settles.

**Section card** — Master B, in time. Section code `§0#` in Space Mono 2.4% H Brass Gold; title Inter 800 5.2% H; precis one sentence Inter 300 2.6% H Muted Blue; `PART 02 / 05` top-right in Space Mono 1.6% H. One large background hex (120–200px, Blueprint Blue at 12%, §7.5). Corner marks. Hold 2.5–3 s.

**End card** — Master F, in time.
- Wordmark + subline centred at 40% frame width; logo mark (§4.2) bottom-centre at 7.4% H (≈80px).
- The thesis line above the wordmark: *"The healthcare system doesn't need to be simpler. It needs to be better understood."* Inter 400, 3.0% H, Paper Light; *"better understood"* may be Inter 300 gold.
- `workingcomplexity.health` in Space Mono 1.8% H below the wordmark. `SCALE 1:1 · ALL DIMENSIONS IN EXPERIENCE` bottom-right, Space Mono 1.6% H Dim Blue — once per video, here only.
- Corner marks. Hold **5 s** minimum.

Corner marks on video cards: 20px legs, 1px stroke, Dim Blue at 40% (§7.2), placed at the safe margin. Never on footage.

### 13.5 Logo bug

None by default. The title and end cards carry the brand. For videos over three minutes that will be clipped and re-shared off-site, an optional bug may be used:
- The logo mark only — never the wordmark — without crosshair, top-right inside the safe margin, **4.4% H** (≈48px), **35% opacity**.
- Hidden on title, section and end cards, and while a lower-third is on screen. Never animated.

### 13.6 Hero numbers and data callouts

Adapted from §11.4. One number per card, on Blueprint Navy.
- Number: Inter 800, **22% H** (≈240px), Brass Gold, left-aligned on the safe margin.
- Label above: Space Mono 400 UPPERCASE, 2.4% H, `0.25em`, Muted Blue.
- Caption below: Inter 400, 3.7% H, Paper Light, one line.
- Source: Space Mono 1.6% H, Dim Blue, bottom-left.
- Motion: a Diagram Blue `#4A90E2` dimension tick draws on beneath the number (400 ms), then the number fades up with a 2% H rise (400 ms). Whole numbers may count up over 600 ms; never count up decimals, percentages with intervals or anything where the intermediate values would imply false precision.
- Charts follow §11.3. Bars grow from the baseline over 400 ms, staggered 3 frames; the gold datum bar arrives last. Hold the finished chart for at least 3 s.

### 13.7 Motion principles

Motion is quiet, precise and purposeful — a blueprint being drawn, not a brand being performed. Every movement must show the viewer where to look next or how two things relate. If it does neither, cut it.

**The vocabulary (use only these):**
1. **Stroke draw-on** — hex lines, rules, dimension ticks, diagram connectors and the logo mark draw along their path.
2. **Opacity fade** — text and solid elements fade from 0 to 100%.
3. **Small translate** — paired with a fade, a rise of at most **2% H** (≈22px), or a slide from the left of at most 2% of frame width for lower-thirds. Never scale graphics.

**Timings:**

| Use | Duration | 25 fps | 30 fps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small text, rules, exits | 200 ms | 5 f | 6 f |
| Lower-third and caption-band exits | 300 ms | 8 f | 9 f |
| Standard text entrance | 400 ms | 10 f | 12 f |
| Stroke draw-on, hero number, mark build | 600 ms | 15 f | 18 f |

- **Easing:** one family only. Entrances `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` (ease-out quint); exits its mirror `cubic-bezier(0.64, 0, 0.78, 0)` (ease-in quint). No linear motion except stroke draw-on, which may run linear.
- **Stagger:** 3 frames between related elements (eyebrow → title → subtitle).
- **One thing moves at a time.** A related group (eyebrow, name, rule) counts as one element and may stagger; two unrelated elements never move together.
- **Settle, then hold.** Everything is still for at least 1.5 s before anything exits. No ambient or looping motion — the hex grid does not drift.
- **The mark build** (title and end cards only): outer hex draws 600 ms → middle hex starts +200 ms → inner hex +200 ms → centre dot fades in 200 ms. About one second in all.
- **Stills:** a documentary still may push in by no more than 3% over the length of the shot, linear. No Ken Burns pans.

**Banned:** bounce, elastic or overshoot easing; spin; zoom-punch; glitch; shake; light leaks; lens flares; whoosh transitions; 3D flips; motion blur trails; typewriter text; and every stock transition — page curl, star wipe, cube spin, iris, clock wipe, push, slide.

**Edits between shots:** **hard cuts** by default. A **dip to Blueprint Navy** is allowed only at a section change: 300 ms out, no more than 12 frames held on navy (the section card may sit here), 300 ms in. No cross-dissolves between shots. J- and L-cuts on audio are encouraged; add a 2-frame audio crossfade at every cut to avoid clicks.

**Why this differs from decks (§11.5).** A deck is read at the reader's pace, with a presenter in the room; motion there competes with the speaker. A video carries its own pace, and the viewer cannot turn back a page. So in video, motion does work a presenter would otherwise do — it orders attention and draws relationships. It is permitted because it carries meaning, and only when it does.

### 13.8 Sound

- **Speech first.** Dialogue normalised to **−16 LUFS** integrated, true peak no higher than −1 dBTP.
- **Music bed (optional):** understated, instrumental, no lyrics, no build-and-drop structure. Ducked to about 18 dB under speech; may rise to −22 LUFS only where nobody is speaking. Fade in over 1 s, out over 2 s.
- **No sound effects** — no whooshes, risers, stingers, clicks or typing sounds under graphics.
- The one exception: an optional single soft tone on the end card, under 1 s, peaking no higher than −20 dBFS.

### 13.9 Colour grading

- Natural, Rec.709. Neutral white balance, true skin tones, highlights and shadows kept rather than clipped or crushed.
- No heavy LUT looks: no teal-and-orange, no bleach bypass, no film-emulation grain, no vignettes.
- Footage must not compete with the palette. Do not push footage towards navy or gold; a slight desaturation (up to −10%) is permitted when footage sits between navy cards.
- The §7.6 duotone (shadows to Blueprint Navy, highlights to Paper Light) is for stills and archive material only — never for live-action interview footage.

### 13.10 Imagery and B-roll

§7.6 applies in full to B-roll.
- Documentary, available-light, unstaged. Hospitals and clinical spaces without actors.
- No stethoscopes, gloved hands, pill bottles, drips in close-up, smiling stock clinicians, doctors pointing at tablets, or people in business attire around a laptop.
- People filmed with dignity — from behind or in their environment, never as "the patient" persona. Patients and carers appear only with consent.
- If suitable B-roll is not available, **do not** use stock. Cut to a hex-language graphic or a diagram (§7.7) instead.

### 13.11 Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Hard cuts between shots | Cross-dissolves, wipes or any stock transition |
| Dip to navy at section changes only | Dip to black, or a dip at every cut |
| Draw lines on; fade text up | Scale, spin, bounce or punch graphics in |
| One element moving at a time | Several things animating at once |
| Verbatim captions on a navy band | Summarised, karaoke or all-caps captions |
| Lower-third once per speaker | Repeating a lower-third each time someone speaks |
| Natural grade, Rec.709 | Teal-and-orange, heavy LUTs, vignettes |
| Speech at −16 LUFS, music ducked under it | Music that competes with the voice; whooshes and stingers |
| Documentary B-roll, or a diagram | Stock healthcare footage |
| Title card and end card carry the brand | A permanent logo bug on every second of footage |
