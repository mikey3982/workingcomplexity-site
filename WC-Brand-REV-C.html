Eight principles
01  ·  Hold complexity

Never reach for "simply" or "just". If something is hard, say so — then show how we work with it.

02  ·  Short, paired statements

WC's signature cadence. "Not simplified. Understood." Two sentences in counterpoint.

03  ·  Concrete over abstract

Name the clinician, the ward, the handoff, the moment. Avoid "stakeholders", "end users", "solutions".

04  ·  UK English

Organisation, centre, behaviour, programme, analyse. Always.

05  ·  Em dashes, used well

Hold two ideas in relation — not as a pause-of-convenience.

06  ·  First person plural

"We" — not "the team" or "WC". The company speaks as a working practice.

Application

Reports. PDFs.

Four artefact families. One voice, one palette, one mark — different formats for different contexts.

WC-BRAND  /  §9–12 APPLICATION  /  REV.C
workingcomplexity.health
§9  ·  Word reports

Long-form written deliverables

  • — A4 portrait · 25mm margins
  • — Light variant (photocopies cleanly)
  • — Body: Inter 11pt 300 · lh 1.5
  • — Bullets: em dash — not dot
  • — References: Harvard author-date
  • — Logo mark 40mm on cover
§10  ·  PDFs

Print-ready or digital-first

  • — Two families: long-form (light) + presentation (dark)
  • — Presentation: 1920×1080 · dark variant
  • — Research report → light
  • — One-pager, leave-behind → dark
  • — Embed all fonts. Never rasterise type.
§11  ·  Decks

Primary client-facing artefact

  • — 1920×1080 · Blueprint Navy
  • — Six masters: Title · Divider · Content · Quote · Diagram · Close
  • — No animations or transitions
  • — 15–25 slides · no pie charts
  • — Hero numbers: Inter 128pt 800 gold
§12  ·  Web

workingcomplexity.health

  • — Plain HTML/CSS/JS · no framework
  • — Netlify hosting · GitHub CI/CD
  • — Dark-first only (no light variant)
  • — Logo mark hero at 65% opacity
  • — Hex honeycomb services section
  • — Update via wc-update-site skill

§13 Quick reference
Always
  • + Dark navy first — light only for print economy
  • + Gold sparingly — one word or one element
  • + Sentence case everywhere except Space Mono labels
  • + Paired statements for headlines
  • + Em dashes and UK English throughout
  • + First person plural ("we")
  • + Named people — never "stakeholders"
  • + Hex grid on covers and dividers — never on body
  • + Pointy-top hexagons, vertically aligned
  • + Inter + Space Mono — no third typeface
Never
  • × Mix dark and light variants in one artefact
  • × Fill large areas in gold
  • × Title case in Inter
  • × Question-mark headlines
  • × Stock clinician-pointing-at-tablet imagery
  • × Recolour, rotate, or fill the logo mark
  • × Pie charts (ever)
  • × Animations or transitions in decks
  • × Corner marks on content slides
  • × Crosshair on logo mark below 48px
